How it works

Calm, then heads up, then enough

The timer moves through three stages as your day goes on. You choose when each one starts, what it is called, and how it looks.

Features

Small, and made to be yours

Over any app

The timer floats above everything you open. Show it all the time, or for a few seconds every minute.

Tap it, drag it

Tap the timer to hide it for 5 seconds. Drag it anywhere on screen and it stays there.

Live settings

Name each stage, pick its text and background colors, size and position. Changes apply the moment you make them.

A daily reset you can trust

Pick the time your day starts over. The reset happens even when the phone is asleep.

Home screen widget

Prefer no overlay at all? Hide the timer and keep an eye on today from a small widget instead.

Take it with you

Export your settings and history to a file, and import them on your next phone.

Analytics

See where your day goes

After sleep and work, how much of the day is really yours? The "Your day" view splits 24 hours so you can see how much of your free time the phone takes.

  • Sleep7h
  • Work and duties8h
  • Free time6h 3m
  • Screen time2h 57m

Alongside it: your daily average, whether you are trending up or down, and a lifetime projection of what your current habit adds up to.
