A new look, built around a calm night in the mountains.

* Redesigned app and a new app icon.
* The home screen shows today's screen time once tracking has started.
* Analytics has a trend chart for week, month or year (opens on month);
  the daily log is tucked away until you need it.
* Settings shows one stage at a time.
* Optional coloured border around the timer.
* The Start/Stop button no longer shifts position.
